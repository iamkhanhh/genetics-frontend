How is the Evidence Score calculated?

The Evidence Score (0–17) is a composite pathogenicity index derived from eight independent in-silico predictors. Higher scores indicate stronger multi-tool evidence for clinical pathogenicity.

Predictor Condition Points Interpretation
CADD PHRED ≥ 30 +3 Top 0.1% most deleterious
≥ 20 +2 Top 1% most deleterious
≥ 10 +1 Above median deleteriousness
VEP Impact HIGH +3 Frameshift, stop-gain, splice-site
MODERATE +1 Missense, in-frame indel
gnomAD Frequency Absent / Novel (0 or N/A) +2 Not observed in population
< 0.001 (0.1%) +1 Very rare in general population
PolyPhen-2 (HDIV) > 0.908 +2 Probably damaging
> 0.446 +1 Possibly damaging
SIFT < 0.05 +1 Deleterious substitution
REVEL ≥ 0.75 +2 Strong pathogenicity evidence
≥ 0.50 +1 Moderate pathogenicity evidence
AlphaMissense likely_pathogenic +2 AI structural pathogenicity prediction
ambiguous +1 Uncertain structural effect
SpliceAI (max Δ score) ≥ 0.5 +2 High splice disruption probability
≥ 0.2 +1 Moderate splice disruption probability
Score Interpretation
12 – 17 Strong multi-tool evidence of pathogenicity
7 – 11 Moderate evidence — warrants further review
0 – 6 Limited in-silico support
This score is for research assistance only. It does not replace clinical interpretation or ACMG/AMP variant classification guidelines.
